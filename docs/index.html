Human Interaction-Aware 3D Reconstruction from a Single Image

HUG3D reconstructs physically plausible, high-fidelity textured 3D humans from a single image, while handling perspective distortion, occlusion, and inter-human interactions.

Abstract

Reconstructing textured 3D humans from a single image is essential for AR/VR and digital human applications, but existing approaches mostly target single-person scenes and often fail under heavy occlusion and close interactions. HUG3D introduces a holistic pipeline that combines canonical perspective-to-orthographic transformation (Pers2Ortho), Human Group-Instance Multi-View Diffusion (HUG-MVD), and physics-aware Human Group-Instance Geometry Reconstruction (HUG-GR) for accurate geometry and high-fidelity texture recovery.

By jointly modeling group-level context and instance-level details, HUG3D recovers plausible inter-human contact, completes occluded regions, and significantly improves reconstruction quality over single-human and prior multi-human baselines.

Method Overview

Stable Canonicalization

Depth-aware filtering stabilizes orthographic projection under severe occlusion and perspective distortion.

Interaction-Aware Diffusion

HUG-MVD jointly models group and instance cues to complete missing geometry and textures consistently.

Texture Fusion

View-aware confidence and edge-aware blending reduce boundary artifacts in reconstructed textures.
